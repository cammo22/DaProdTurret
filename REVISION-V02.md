- Weapons spend ammunition, NEVER energy. Magazine auto-reloads from unlimited reserve; reload is explicit, visible and upgradeable. Energy remains for EMP and missiles. Show ammo/reload, HP and energy directly beneath the turret, anchored to its projected world position.
- EMP stuns affected ships (movement AND firing stop), with visible electrical/hit feedback per ship, not just a ring. A separate missile-launcher ability launches real homing projectiles with trails/explosions.
- Cannon needs visible traveling shells, muzzle flash, recoil and hit splash. Tesla bounces sequentially with a clearly visible ricochet from ship to ship. Railgun has a visible timed charge, then a satisfying heavy beam/recoil/piercing. Each weapon has its own internal upgrade tree.

- Remove ALL research/prestige/extraction/offline salvage. On defeat lose run, only persistent highscore/bestWave remain. Restart fresh with initial upgrades and scrap.
- Add ranged ships that stand off and launch incoming shells, submarines that dive/surface and fire torpedoes, boss with prominent health bar, clickable sea events (supply/repair/ammo/reward crate) plus visible incoming threats.
- Audio DEFAULT ON (browser starts AudioContext on first gesture; never promise autoplay before gesture).
- NO sidebar/console/dashboard. Sea/game viewport full screen with compact diegetic-style overlay controls. Upgrades are an in-game anchored dock/radial panel near turret, not a page sidebar. Compact clickable weapon/ability icons at screen edge; manual targeting must remain available. Minimal score/wave top left, boss HP top center, utility pause/sound corner. No compass, no sector/outpost/coordinates/flavor slogans/connection status/record banners as decoration.
- Mobile portrait and landscape fit the viewport: no document scrolling for upgrades or abilities; opening compact upgrade overlay pauses game and closes to resume. touch targets >=44px. Use short literal labels only.
- Use actual generated Meshy meshes visibly for cannon/boats/submarine/boss, keep moving weapon assemblies independent.
